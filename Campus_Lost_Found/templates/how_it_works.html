A SMALL ACTION. A HAPPY RETURN.

From lost to returned.

Three steps to reconnect a campus item with its owner.

  1. Search first

    Use a title, category or status to find a matching notice. Open it and check the location and distinguishing details.

  2. Share the details

    Post a Lost or Found notice with a useful title, category, date and location. Keep names, ID numbers and other private details out of the description.

  3. Close the loop

    Arrange a safe return. The notice owner can edit the notice and mark it as returned. The status is always shown as text.

How the prototype saves your work

Local browser → Flask and permission checks → SQLite database → updated notice. Data remains available after the app restarts.

Quiet and accessible

No sound plays. Status text carries every result, including successful publication and return. The short confirmation animation respects your system's reduced-motion preference. Category photos are illustrative; read the notice to identify the actual item.
