The frozen dataset contains 122 synthetic notices: 96 open and 26 marked returned, comprising 60 lost and 62 found notices. Eight categories each have 12 open notices; Electronics and Drinkware have none. The generator strongly shapes this pattern: all 12 Electronics notices and all 13 Drinkware notices are marked returned. It cannot establish that these categories are easier to recover.

Library study area has the largest location total, with 13 notices (12 lost and 1 found). Locations are free-form labels, so "Library study area" and "Library, second floor" remain separate groups. Normalising location choices would make future comparisons more consistent. Notice counts also do not identify unique physical items or account for how many people use each location.

Weekly totals reach 21 in four consecutive September weeks. This reflects the seed schedule and later demonstration edits, not observed campus demand. Weeks are grouped by item event date, not publication, processing or return time; boundary weeks have partial date coverage. These aggregates verify the dashboard and suggest interface improvements, while real service evaluation requires independent observations.
