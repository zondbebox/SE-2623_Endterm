CAMPUS LOST & FOUND — PARTICIPANT TASK CARDS
Use one participant code for both tasks. All items and accounts are fictional.
The observer starts and stops the timer. You may stop at any time.
Please work as you normally would. We are testing the interface, not you.

TASK 1 — PUBLISH A FOUND NOTICE
Starting point: you are signed in as a student and see Browse notices.

Imagine you found a blue notebook in the library today. Publish a found-item notice so its owner can identify it.
Use these fictional details:
Title: Blue notebook T01 (replace T01 with your participant code)
Category: Books and Stationery
Type: Found
Location: Library, second floor
Date: today's date
Description: A blue notebook with a small star sticker was found near the library. This is a fictional usability-test item with no personal details.
A photograph is not required for this task.

Tell the observer when you believe the notice has been published.
Finish condition: the saved notice is visible with the correct title and information.
The observer records the actual time; the project target is completion within 120 seconds.
Do not use real identifying information or a real university password.

TASK 2 — FIND THE TARGET NOTEBOOK
Starting point: Browse notices, with the search box and filters cleared.

Imagine you lost a red notebook near the computer lab corridor.
Find the existing found-item notice for that notebook. Its category is Books and Stationery.
Open its details and tell the observer its title and where it was found.
You may use any controls in the application.

Finish condition: the correct Red notebook details are open and you identify Computer lab corridor.
The observer records the actual time; the project target is completion within 60 seconds.
